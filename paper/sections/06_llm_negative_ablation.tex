We tested whether augmenting DHRP with FinBERT \citep{Huang2023}
embeddings of asset-attributed news headlines could improve portfolio
allocation. Under multi-seed evaluation, the answer is: no.

\paragraph{Setup.} For each of the 8 universes, we trained both DHRP and
LLM-DHRP across the same 10 seeds. Text features are 90-day rolling
windows of asset-attributed headlines, FinBERT-encoded, L2-normalized
per asset, and aggregated as concatenated mean+max
(Section~\ref{sec:llm_dhrp_arch}). LLM-DHRP uses warm-start training:
phase~1 freezes the price pathway and only trains text/fusion;
phase~2 fine-tunes everything with reduced learning rate
(Appendix~\ref{app:hyperparams}).

\paragraph{Why does LLM augmentation fail?}
We investigated three candidate explanations:

\textbf{(1)~Per-asset headline coverage sparsity.} Despite a 90-day
rolling window, the average asset has news in only $\sim$40\% of
windows; the rest fall back to a market-wide embedding. The result is
that per-asset variation in the text tensor is dominated by missing-
data fallback rather than asset-specific signal.

\textbf{(2)~BERT anisotropy persistence after temporal aggregation.}
Per-headline FinBERT embeddings have moderate cosine similarity
(mean $0.24$, std $0.14$) ---  the model layer is fine. But after
temporal+L2-normalized $[\text{mean}, \text{max}]$ aggregation,
per-timestep cosine similarity rises to $0.96$ with std $0.04$,
indicating the aggregation collapses inter-day variation.

\textbf{(3)~Warm-start training instability.} The cross-modal gate
amplifies small text signals into large weight swings (mean $L_1$ weight
diff with vs.\ without text $= 0.61$ in DM). Combined with the high
seed-to-seed variance (std $0.121$ in commodities, vs.\ $0.067$ for
DHRP), this suggests the text pathway over-fits in-sample and
generalizes poorly.

\paragraph{Comparison to literature.}
This negative result is consistent with concurrent benchmark evidence:
\citet{Tan2024AreLMs} (NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight) showed that removing the
LLM component from time-series forecasters improves performance;
\citet{Chen2025StockBench} demonstrated most LLM-trading agents
underperform buy-and-hold; \citet{Xie2024FinBen} reported LLMs are
weak at forecasting. Single-seed claims of LLM-augmentation lifts in
finance \citep{Kirtac2025} should be interpreted with caution. We
conjecture that text features may help on tasks where the dominant
information channel is news (e.g., event-driven trading), but not on
broad portfolio allocation where covariance structure already captures
most relevant cross-asset signal.

\paragraph{Implication.} The headline DHRP results
(Sections~\ref{sec:results_main}--\ref{sec:results_alphas}) stand
without LLM augmentation. Future work could explore (a) per-asset news
coverage normalization, (b) sentence-transformer embeddings tuned for
finance \citep{FinLang2025}, or (c) gated mixture-of-experts routing
that conditionally activates text only when news velocity exceeds a
threshold.
