\section{Limitations and Discussion}
\label{sec:limits}

% --- 0.5 page ---

\paragraph{When does DHRP win?}
DHRP achieves significant alpha in commodities (PSR $0.999$) and crypto
(PSR $0.991$), and is the best risk-parity-family method in DM, EM,
sectors, and factors (PSR $\geq 0.86$ in each). It underperforms
classical mean-variance in global multi-asset and EM, where MV's
closed-form solution exploits factor exposures more efficiently than
soft-routing through a binary tree. The pattern suggests DHRP's
hierarchical inductive bias is most valuable when (i)~average pairwise
correlation is low ($<0.5$ in commodities and crypto vs.\ $>0.7$ in
DM/EM equities) and (ii)~the cross-asset structure is regime-dependent
rather than factor-dominated. In the bonds universe, all methods bleed
during the 2022--2023 rate-hike cycle (PSR $\approx 0$), so the
underperformance is universe-level rather than method-specific.

\paragraph{Failed hypotheses.}
We hypothesized that LLM augmentation would lift performance in
commodity and crypto markets where narrative arguably matters more.
The data does not support this under multi-seed reporting
(Section~\ref{sec:llmablation}). We also hypothesized that deeper trees
would capture finer cross-asset structure; ablation
(Appendix~\ref{app:additional}) shows depth$=$3 is optimal in DM but
the relationship is not stable across runs, suggesting current text
and price features do not provide enough signal to support deeper
gating networks.

\paragraph{Threats to validity.}
\textit{Survivorship bias.} Our ETF universes are static (no delisting
adjustments); a small bias toward survived ETFs is documented in
Appendix~\ref{app:croissant} as part of the Responsible-AI metadata.
\textit{Backtest overfitting.} We use the Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio
\citep{Bailey2012PSR} but not the Deflated Sharpe \citep{Bailey2014DSR},
which would adjust for the implicit hyperparameter search across
universes; we do not view this as material because we report
out-of-sample numbers across all universes from the same protocol.
\textit{Single-period OOS.} The OOS window 2020-07 to 2026-04 spans the
COVID crash, recovery, rate-hike cycle, and post-hike regime.
Results are robust within sub-periods (Appendix~\ref{app:additional})
but a longer hold-out would strengthen claims.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Three directions: (i)~theoretical generalization bounds via covering
numbers of the soft-gating tree (informal sketch in
Appendix~\ref{app:proof}); (ii)~per-asset news coverage normalization to
revisit LLM augmentation in commodity and crypto markets; and
(iii)~combining DHRP with $m$-sparse selection \citep{Lin2024MSparse}
to recover competitiveness in high-correlation equity universes.
