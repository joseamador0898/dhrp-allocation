\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% --- 0.25 page ---

We introduced \textbf{DHRP}, a differentiable extension of Hierarchical
Risk Parity that recovers classical HRP as routing temperature
$\tau \to 0$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:recovery}) while enabling
end-to-end gradient training, and \textbf{DHRP-8}, the first
multi-universe portfolio benchmark with multi-seed Probabilistic Sharpe
Ratio reporting across eight asset classes and eleven methods. DHRP
achieves significant out-of-sample alpha in commodities (FF3 $t=2.49$,
AQR $t=4.00$, PSR $0.99$) and the highest Sharpe in crypto, beating
classical HRP in five of eight universes. We document an honest
negative result: under multi-seed reporting, FinBERT-based LLM
augmentation provides no statistical lift and regresses in commodities,
contrasting with single-seed claims in the recent literature.

The benchmark, code, model checkpoints across 10 seeds, and Croissant 1.1
metadata are released as supplementary materials. We hope DHRP-8 will
serve as a calibrated reference for future work on differentiable
portfolio optimization and provide the community with a structured
testbed for honest evaluation of new methods.
