\subsection{Multi-universe Sharpe summary}
\label{sec:results_main}

Table~\ref{tab:headline} reports the headline result. DHRP achieves
significant Sharpe with PSR $> 0.95$ in commodities, crypto, sectors
(borderline 0.946), and factors (borderline 0.949), and is the best
risk-parity-family method in DM ($0.446 \pm 0.06$ vs.\ HRP $0.216$,
EW $0.255$). Mean-variance dominates the global multi-asset universe
(Sharpe $0.99$, PSR $0.99$); all methods bleed during the bonds bear
period. The full results are auto-generated from
\texttt{results/sharpe\_pivot\_multiseed\_*.csv} via

\subsection{Factor regression alphas}
\label{sec:results_alphas}

In the commodity universe, DHRP achieves a Fama--French 3-factor
annualized alpha of $16.1\%$ ($t{=}2.49$, $p{=}0.013$) and an AQR
cross-sectional Value/Momentum alpha of $20.7\%$ ($t{=}4.00$,
$p{<}0.001$). Both are the strongest in the universe; LLM-DHRP also
achieves significant FF3 alpha ($13.1\%$, $t{=}2.06$, $p{=}0.039$) but
ranks below DHRP. Auto-generated table below; full per-factor breakdown
