\section{The DHRP-8 Benchmark}
\label{sec:benchmark}

% --- 1.5 pages ---

DHRP-8 evaluates portfolio allocation methods across eight asset
universes, eleven baselines, and a rigorous statistical battery. This
section is the E\&D track contribution: a calibrated, multi-seed,
multi-universe benchmark that the community can build on.

We selected universes spanning equity (DM, EM, Sectors, Factors), credit
(Bonds), commodities, and alternatives (Crypto, Global multi-asset) to
expose distinct correlation, factor, and regime structures. All price
data are downloaded from Yahoo Finance v8 API and cached locally;
volume data and Fama--French \citep{FamaFrench2015} factors are
obtained via standard public sources. Rebalance frequency and lookback
windows are tuned per asset class based on regime half-life
(commodities and crypto: 63-day lookback to capture shorter-cycle
news-sentiment premium; equities and bonds: 252-day annual cycle).

\subsection{Baselines}
\label{sec:baselines}

We evaluate eleven methods spanning six classical, three deep, and two
hierarchical:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Classical}: Equal-weight (EW), Mean-Variance (MV),
    Minimum-Variance (MINVAR), Risk Parity (RP), Maximum
    Diversification (MAXDIV), Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP).
  \item \textbf{Deep}: an MLP with covariance-projection head, a
    Portfolio Transformer \citep{KisielGorse2022}, and a
    Proximal Policy Optimization agent (PPO).
  \item \textbf{This paper}: DHRP, LLM-DHRP, and a Decision-Focused
    Learning baseline (DFL) that mirrors recent ICAIF 2025 work
    \citep{KimLee2025DFL}.
\end{itemize}
All deep methods are trained with identical optimizer, scheduler, and
seed protocols (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}).

\subsection{Multi-seed protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

% TODO: 0.5 page - this is the E&D contribution
For every neural method (DHRP, LLM-DHRP, MLP, Transformer, PPO, DFL) we
train independently across $S = 10$ random seeds
$\{0, 1, \ldots, 9\}$. Each model is backtested over the OOS window
2020-07-01 to 2026-04-15 with rolling rebalance at the
universe-specific frequency. Per-method statistics are reported as
mean$\pm$std across seeds; classical baselines (EW, MV, MINVAR, HRP, RP,
MAXDIV) are deterministic and reported as a single value. Models are
trained on the in-sample period 2012--2020-06; OOS evaluation begins
after a 5-day purge gap to prevent train/test leakage.

\subsection{Statistical battery}
\label{sec:stats}

DHRP-8 reports five families of significance tests:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio (PSR)}
    \citep{Bailey2012PSR}: probability the true Sharpe exceeds zero,
    sample-corrected for skew/kurtosis. PSR $> 0.95$ implies
    significance at the 5\% level.
  \item \textbf{Newey--West HAC} t-statistics on annualized excess
    returns \citep{NeweyWest1987}.
  \item \textbf{Factor regression alphas}: Fama--French 3 and 5 factors
    \citep{FamaFrench2015}, momentum \citep{Carhart1997}, and AQR
    commodity Value/Momentum factors \citep{AsnessMoskowitzPedersen2013}
    for the commodity universe.
  \item \textbf{Pairwise tests vs.\ HRP}: stationary block bootstrap
    \citep{PolitisRomano1994} on Sharpe differences with
    Holm--Bonferroni correction \citep{Holm1979}; Diebold--Mariano
    forecast accuracy \citep{DieboldMariano1995}.
  \item \textbf{Multi-method tests}: Superior Predictive Ability
    \citep{Hansen2005} and Model Confidence Set
    \citep{HansenLundeNason2011} at $\alpha=0.05$.
\end{enumerate}
Transaction-cost sensitivity at $\{0, 5, 10, 20, 50\}$ bps and
sub-period analysis (5 macro regimes) are reported in
Appendix~\ref{app:additional}.

\subsection{Reproducibility and dataset release}
\label{sec:repro}

We release the DHRP-8 benchmark with Croissant~1.1 metadata
\citep{Akhtar2024Croissant} including license, schema, train/test
splits, and Responsible-AI annotations covering survivorship bias and
data-leakage warnings (Appendix~\ref{app:croissant}).
Anonymized GitHub link, frozen pip requirements, and a single-command
\texttt{reproduce\_all.sh} pipeline are included with the supplementary
materials. Each individual experiment is also reproducible by setting
the seed and universe in the configuration file.
